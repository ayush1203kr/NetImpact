# NetImpact Recommendations

1. Investigate retention first. Active users declined 7.60% and unsubscribed users increased 57.77%; segment both metrics by city, plan, and month.
2. Review declining or discontinued plans. Understand why Legacy 249 was discontinued and whether customers moved to another plan or left.
3. Evaluate the new Ultra 5G 899 plan. Track active users, ARPU, and unsubscribes before expanding it.
4. Use city-specific actions. Compare cities with high revenue and weaker change percentages; investigate coverage, service quality, and plan mix.
5. Protect premium revenue carefully. Confirm that higher ARPU is not masking customer loss.
6. Add a monthly retention review using the monthly trend and Power BI slicers.

These are practical next steps supported by the analysis. Operational data such as network quality, customer complaints, and campaign history would be needed before final decisions.
