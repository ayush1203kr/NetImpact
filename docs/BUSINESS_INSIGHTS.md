# NetImpact Business Insights

These findings come from the deterministic synthetic data generated by `sql/01_create_database.sql`. They are observations, not proof that 5G caused any change.

## 1. Revenue increased modestly

Revenue was INR 2,124,567,603.28 before 5G and INR 2,339,397,932.56 after 5G, a 10.11% increase.

## 2. Active users declined

Active users fell from 2,424,556 to 2,240,246, a 7.60% decrease.

## 3. Unsubscribed users increased

Unsubscribed users rose from 75,300 to 118,800, a 57.77% increase.

## 4. ARPU improved

ARPU increased from INR 876.27 to INR 1,044.26.

## 5. City performance varies

City-level revenue changes differ across markets. This supports investigating local coverage, plan mix, and retention rather than applying one national action.

## 6. Plans show concentration and lifecycle movement

Premium plans contribute substantial revenue, while Legacy 249 has no after-5G activity and Ultra 5G 899 is a newly introduced plan with after-5G revenue.

For each insight, treat the result as an observation and investigate operational causes before making a final business decision.
